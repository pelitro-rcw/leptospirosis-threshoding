# RF Upper-Tail and A1/A2 False-Alarm Concept

All RF products use an upper-tail environmental activation rule (`RF >= threshold`). The environmental feature is computed from the four complete weeks preceding the disease-anchor week (`t-4` through `t-1`), excluding the disease-trigger week.

The RF threshold does **not** create a new truth definition. Constant TA and Outbreak Threshold onset events remain classified against the established leptospirosis anticipatory reference framework:

- **A1**: 4–8 weeks before the annual leptospirosis peak.
- **A2**: the contiguous period containing the peak and at least 70% of annual leptospirosis burden.
- **True alarm**: disease-anchor onset in A1 **or** A2.
- **False alarm**: disease-anchor onset outside A1 and A2.

Candidate RF thresholds qualify disease-anchor onsets. Threshold selection then evaluates the resulting A1/A2-defined true and false alarms. Low rainfall below the lower operational bound is classified as no RF activation and cannot be labelled a strong trigger.

NCR thresholds are fixed within NCR after NCR-only derivation with LOYO stability. Regional thresholds are fixed for the pooled 17-region framework with LORO stability.
