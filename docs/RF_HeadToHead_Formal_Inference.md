# RF mm versus RF STA/LTA R(t): formal head-to-head inference

Stage H formally compares the two rainfall representations within the same disease anchor:

- Product 1 (RF mm) versus Product 3 (empirical RF STA/LTA R(t)) for Constant TA.
- Product 2 (RF mm) versus Product 4 (empirical RF STA/LTA R(t)) for Outbreak Threshold.

## Primary inferential unit

NCR is paired by evaluable year. Regional analysis treats region as the cluster and pairs the two RF representations within each of the 17 regions. The formal paired test is an exact two-sided sign-flip randomization test of the paired difference. Paired percentile-bootstrap 95% confidence intervals are generated by resampling the paired year or regional units. Eight prespecified metrics are tested and Bonferroni correction is applied separately within each anchor and spatial scale.

The direction of benefit is prespecified. Higher values are favorable for TAM, true alarms, PPV, sensitivity, mean lead time, warning persistence and ALY. Lower false-alarm burden is favorable.

## Threshold stability and operational bounds

The adopted point estimate remains the primary operational RF threshold used in headline performance. Stage H does not treat the lower and upper empirical stability bounds as independent observations. Instead, it compares lower, point and upper threshold operating characteristics as a robustness diagnostic and reports scope-specific threshold stability: LOYO for NCR and LORO for Regional analysis.

## Dominance probability

For each anchor and spatial scale, the direction-adjusted paired metric differences are standardized within metric and averaged within the paired unit. Paired units are bootstrap-resampled to estimate the probability that RF STA/LTA R(t) or RF mm has positive overall dominance.

## Overall conclusion

The pipeline does not force a winner. `RF_HeadToHead_OverallDecision.csv` reports one of:

- RF mm preferred
- RF STA/LTA R(t) preferred
- No clear empirical superiority

A representation is preferred only when it has majority directional superiority across the eight metrics, no Bonferroni-significant deterioration, either at least one Bonferroni-significant favorable metric or dominance probability of at least 0.75, and no conflicting majority across lower/point/upper balanced-accuracy sensitivity checks.
